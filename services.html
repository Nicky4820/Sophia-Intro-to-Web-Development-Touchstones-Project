Adoption Process

  1. Review available pets and consider which animal fits your household.
  2. Submit an adoption inquiry so our team can learn about your goals and experience.
  3. Talk with a rescue coordinator about the pet's needs, personality, and care requirements.
  4. Meet the pet and complete the adoption steps recommended by the rescue team.
  5. Bring your new companion home and continue building a safe, lasting relationship.

Foster Program

Foster families give rescue pets a safe temporary home while the organization works toward adoption. Fosters help pets adjust, learn household routines, and show their personalities to potential adopters.

Foster interest is especially valuable when an animal needs extra time, attention, or a quiet environment.

Volunteer Roles

  • Animal Care: Assist with feeding, enrichment, cleaning, and basic daily care.
  • Adoption Events: Help welcome visitors and support meet-and-greet events.
  • Transport: Help move pets and supplies safely when transportation needs arise.
  • Community Outreach: Share rescue information and help connect neighbors with resources.
  • Administrative Support: Assist with organizing information, supplies, and community communications.
