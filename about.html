Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began with a simple community goal: help animals in need while giving local people practical ways to make a difference. Today, our work brings together adopters, foster families, volunteers, donors, and community partners around the shared goal of giving rescue pets a better future.

We believe successful rescue work is built on trust, responsible care, and strong relationships within the community.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal and every person with patience and respect.
  • Responsibility: We make decisions with the safety and long-term welfare of animals in mind.
  • Community: We believe lasting change happens when neighbors work together.
  • Transparency: We communicate honestly about our work, needs, and impact.
  • Action: We turn concern for animals into concrete opportunities to help.

Community Partners
