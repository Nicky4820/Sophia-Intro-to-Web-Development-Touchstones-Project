Every Pet Deserves a Second Chance

We connect homeless and at-risk companion animals with caring people throughout the Twin Cities. Whether you are ready to adopt, foster, volunteer, donate, or partner with us, your action can make a difference.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide compassionate care for animals in need and build lasting connections between rescue pets and people in our community.

Our Impact

More than 500 pets supported each year.

More than 300 adoption connections made each year.

More than 100 community volunteers and foster families engaged.

How You Can Help

  • Adopt: Give a rescue pet a permanent home.
  • Foster: Provide temporary care while a pet waits for adoption.
  • Volunteer: Help with animal care, events, outreach, or administrative tasks.
  • Donate: Help provide food, medical care, enrichment, and supplies.
  • Partner: Connect your business or community group with local rescue work.
